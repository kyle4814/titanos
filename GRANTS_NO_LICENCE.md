## AUSTRALIAN FEDERAL — mostly disqualifying, explained plainly

### R&D Tax Incentive (R&DTI)
**NOT LIVE VERIFIED this session (403 on ato.gov.au and business.gov.au) — but this is a long-standing statutory scheme and the entity rule below is stable and well documented.**
- **DISQUALIFYING for a sole trader.** The R&DTI is only available to an "R&D entity" as defined in the tax law (ITAA 1997 s355-35), which is restricted to **body corporates** (companies) — an individual or sole trader operating under their own ABN is not an eligible R&D entity, full stop. This is the single most important fact in this report for a sole trader: **you cannot claim the R&D Tax Incentive without incorporating.** Confirm the current wording at https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/rd-tax-incentive before treating this as final, but the "must be a company" rule has been a fixed feature of the scheme for its entire life.
- **Verdict: DISQUALIFYING as a sole trader. Would require incorporating first.**
