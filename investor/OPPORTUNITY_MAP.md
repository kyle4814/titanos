# TitanOS — Investment & Opportunity Map

This is an opportunity map, not a recommendation to invest and not evidence that any financing exists.

## Technical infrastructure lane

**Thesis to test:** evidence-first primitives become useful infrastructure for AI and automation.

Potential capital use:
- API/SDK hardening
- provenance and evidence storage
- developer tooling
- integrations
- benchmarks

Validation:
- external integrations
- repeatable performance measurements
- documented deployment economics

## Verification portal lane

**Thesis to test:** people and organisations have a practical need to check AI-generated or user-supplied information before acting on it.

Potential capital use:
- web interface
- claim extraction
- evidence retrieval
- provenance UI
- human-review workflows

Validation:
- task completion
- accuracy by domain
- false-positive / false-negative analysis
- repeat usage

## Enterprise governance lane

**Thesis to test:** organisations will deploy an evidence gate around consequential automation.

Potential capital use:
- policy engine
- audit trails
- access controls
- private deployment
- integrations

Validation:
- pilot workflows
- prevented or surfaced failures
- deployment cost
- customer willingness to pay

## Research / simulation lane

**Thesis to test:** explicit separation between observations, assumptions and simulations is useful for research and decision support.

Potential capital use:
- experiment runner
- scenario comparison
- provenance graph
- reproducibility tooling

Validation:
- reproducible studies
- independent critique
- measurable reduction in assumption/observation confusion

## Open-source ecosystem lane

**Thesis to test:** a broad, inspectable repository can attract contributors, integrations and independent evaluation.

Potential capital use:
- documentation
- contributor tooling
- test infrastructure
- source adapters
- public benchmarks

Validation:
- external contributors
- independent forks/integrations
- reproducible external experiments
- issue/PR activity

## Grants / public-good lane

Potential fit may include open-source security, trustworthy AI, research infrastructure, digital public goods and defensive tooling.

Each opportunity must be verified against its current eligibility rules before submission. No opportunity is considered secured until an external award is actually evidenced.

## Angel / strategic investment lane

Possible investor-facing materials:
- technical evidence
- architecture
- failure archive
- experiments
- milestones
- market hypotheses
- capital allocation gates
- founder / operator information supplied separately

Investment materials should remain evidence-first. No invented traction, valuation, market size, customer count or return forecast.
