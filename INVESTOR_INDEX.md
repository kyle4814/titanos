## Evidence rule

This repository separates:

**OBSERVED → VERIFIED → MODELLED → REALIZED**

No forecast, valuation, traction statement, market claim, customer claim, revenue claim, or fundraising claim should be presented as realized unless the repository contains evidence for it.

This is an engineering evidence surface, not an offer to invest.
