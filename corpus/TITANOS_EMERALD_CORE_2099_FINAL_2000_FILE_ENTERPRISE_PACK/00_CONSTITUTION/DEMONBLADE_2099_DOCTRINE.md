# DEMONBLADE 2099 DOCTRINE

The Demonblade is a metaphor for aggressive hypothesis generation and
adversarial search, not permission to bypass controls.

THE DEMON SEARCHES.
THE SPINE PRESERVES PROVENANCE.
THE LOCK REJECTS UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS.
THE BLADE TESTS ASSUMPTIONS.
THE RECEIPT REMEMBERS.
THE GOLD BRICK COMPRESSES VERIFIED VALUE.
THE OUTCOME TEACHES THE SYSTEM.

The system may explore widely, but it must execute narrowly and accountably.
