# DEMONBLADE DYNASTY — 2099 VISION

2099 is a long-horizon engineering mythology for accumulated capability.

The dynasty is not a throne and not a claim of inevitability.

It is the body of:
- systems,
- discoveries,
- failures,
- receipts,
- engineering decisions,
- commercial relationships,
- knowledge,
- and lessons converted into durable infrastructure.

The desired inheritance is a higher starting floor for whoever comes next.

The test is simple:

BUILD SOMETHING WORTH LEAVING BEHIND.
