# ABOUT TITANOS

TitanOS is an independent long-horizon engineering project founded and operated
by Kyle Montrose Deligny.

Its philosophy is simple:

ONE PERSON SHOULD BE ABLE TO BUILD MORE THAN ONE PERSON COULD BEFORE.

But the mechanism is not magic. It is disciplined decomposition, search-before-
build, reusable infrastructure, bounded AI agents, durable memory, evidence,
testing, receipts, and relentless iteration.

The project is intentionally ambitious while keeping its claims conservative:
capability must be demonstrated rather than merely described.

TitanOS is the system.
Demonblade is the adversarial engineering philosophy.
The Gold Brick is the unit of verified value.
The Receipt is the chain of evidence.
The Outcome is reality speaking back.
