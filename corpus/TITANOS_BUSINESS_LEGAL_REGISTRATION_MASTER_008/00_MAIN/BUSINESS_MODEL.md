# TITANOS — BUSINESS MODEL

## Core commercial loop
PROBLEM → RECON → DESIGN → BUILD → VERIFY → RECEIPT → GOLD BRICK → LICENSE / DELIVER → REUSE → UPGRADE

## Offers
1. AI engineering and automation
2. AI systems architecture
3. Custom agent / workflow implementation
4. Gold Brick capability licensing
5. Enterprise capability subscriptions
6. Repository / knowledge-system implementation
7. Verification and optimisation services
8. Partner / referral arrangements subject to lawful terms

## Target customers
- SMEs
- tradies and service businesses
- professional services
- technology companies
- enterprise teams
- research organisations
- infrastructure operators
- organisations seeking AI automation
- founders and engineering teams

## Revenue status
No revenue is asserted by this package. Revenue begins only when actual paid transactions are recorded and reconciled.

## What customers buy
Customers buy defined outcomes, software, services, licences or access—not a promise of investment returns.
