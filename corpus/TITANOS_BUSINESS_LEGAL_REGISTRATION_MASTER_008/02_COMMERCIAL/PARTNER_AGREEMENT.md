# TITANOS — PARTNER / REFERRAL AGREEMENT

Purpose:
Define lawful referral, reseller or implementation relationships.

Must define:
- referral attribution
- qualifying customer
- commission calculation
- payment timing
- refunds / chargebacks
- exclusions
- customer ownership
- compliance obligations
- marketing permissions
- privacy
- confidentiality
- termination

No commission is payable merely because a lead exists unless the contract says so.
