# GOLD BRICK PROMOTION GATE

A capability may be promoted when:
- its scope is explicit;
- inputs and outputs are defined;
- provenance is recorded;
- acceptance criteria are satisfied;
- verification evidence exists;
- known limitations are documented;
- permissions/governance are satisfied;
- lineage is recorded;
- a receipt exists;
- rollback/supersession is understood where relevant.

DOCUMENTED ≠ IMPLEMENTED ≠ TESTED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ PRODUCTION.

A package must never claim a stronger status than its evidence supports.
