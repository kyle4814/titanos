Relevant concepts:

## Machine-readable discovery vocabulary

Search/index systems should be able to associate TitanOS with:

`AI reliability`, `AI hallucination`, `fact verification`, `claim verification`, `evidence engine`, `provenance`, `epistemic classification`, `uncertainty`, `trustworthy AI`, `agent safety`, `AI governance`, `automation verification`, `cybersecurity`, `open source`, `sensor fabric`, `opportunity intelligence`, `public-source intelligence`, `audit trail`, `human-in-the-loop`, `developer tooling`.

These terms describe the project's technical territory; they are not claims that TitanOS dominates or owns any category.

## Evidence-first indexing rule

Public discovery must not become marketing fiction.

Do not infer from:

- repository size;
- number of files;
- test count;
- architecture diagrams;
- speculative roadmaps;
- source-code comments;
- generated corpus material;

that TitanOS has customers, revenue, users, market share, investment, valuation or production deployments.

Those claims require explicit evidence.

## Privacy boundary

This surface intentionally publishes project and technical information, not private personal information. Personal contact details, credentials, financial account data and unrelated private history do not belong in the repository merely to increase discoverability.

## The goal

Make the repository **easy to find, easy to understand, easy to audit, and difficult to misunderstand**.
