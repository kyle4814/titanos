## 2. Zero Day Initiative (ZDI) — the standing market, most depth as instructed

This is a **year-round vulnerability acquisition program**, not a competition — no deadline, no seat limit, no travel required. Rated #1 in the ranked table because it has zero calendar risk and the lowest barrier of everything researched.

**How it works**
1. Create a researcher account, submit via the portal: https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/portal/login/
2. Submission can be "sample exploit code, a detailed description of the vulnerability" — no fixed format required
3. TrendAI team follows up if they need more detail; average response time ~2 weeks
4. Researcher can accept or decline whatever compensation offer ZDI makes — **ownership of the submission stays with the researcher until an offer is accepted**, meaning a declined offer isn't a forced sale
5. If accepted: tax documentation required, then payment

**Payout structure**
- No published flat per-bug price list was found; ZDI states valuation depends on "product deployment, privilege gained, default exposure, product importance, and other technical factors" — i.e. it's negotiated/assessed per submission, not a fixed table.
- Separate from per-bug payment, there's a **tiered loyalty bonus** on top, based on cumulative accepted-dollar points (1 accepted dollar = 1 point):
  | Tier | Points needed | Bonus |
  |---|---|---|
  | Bronze | 15,000 | $2,000 one-time + 10% submission bonus |
  | Silver | 25,000 | $5,000 one-time + 15% submission bonus |
  | Gold | 45,000 | $10,000 one-time + 20% submission bonus |
  | Platinum | 65,000 | $25,000 one-time + 25% submission bonus |
- Referral bonus: 2,500 points for bringing in a new researcher who gets accepted
- **No submission cap** — can report unlimited vulnerabilities

**Eligibility (quoted)**
- "independent researchers, academics, private labs, and corporate security teams globally" — explicitly open to individuals, no company/licence/insurance requirement found in the fetched pages.
- Geographic exclusion: "cannot accept researchers from Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Sudan, or Syria due to U.S. law limitations." Australia is not excluded.

**Payment / tax handling**
- Method: bank wire transfer or mailed cheque, researcher's choice; processing "two to three weeks."
- US taxpayers must file a signed W-9 before payment.
- **Non-US researchers**: the fetched FAQ did **not** specify the equivalent form (e.g. W-8BEN is standard practice for US-sourced income paid to foreign persons, but this was not confirmed on ZDI's own page) — marked **UNKNOWN**, do not assume W-8BEN applies without confirming directly with ZDI at submission time.

**Sources**: https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/how-to-work-with-zdi/ , https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/about/benefits/ , https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/about/faq/
